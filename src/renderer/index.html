Connect to Liongard

Three steps, about two minutes. The app reads from Liongard. It never changes anything there.

1

Your Liongard instance

.app.liongard.com

The part of your Liongard sign-in address before .app.liongard.com, such as us1, eu1 or au1. Liongard documentation

2

Access key and secret

Read-only key saved, encrypted on this computer.

Create a read-only access key in Liongard, under your account settings in API keys.

The key is stored encrypted on this computer and is only ever sent to Liongard.

3

Pick a client

Shown after you connect. Each Liongard environment is one client.
